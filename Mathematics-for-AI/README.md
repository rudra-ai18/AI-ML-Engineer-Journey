## Rules

- Do not learn formulas without understanding what they mean.
- Implement important algorithms before relying on library black boxes.
- Every major topic must have at least one visualization or coding experiment.
- Every major topic must be connected to an AI/ML use case.
- Keep daily notes and commit progress consistently.
